BMI Calculator

A fast, clean, offline-first Android app that helps you calculate your Body Mass Index (BMI), see the healthy weight range for your height, and keep a private history on your own device.

1. What Is BMI Calculator?

BMI Calculator is a simple and reliable Android tool made for everyday use. Whether you prefer Metric units (centimeters and kilograms) or Imperial units (feet, inches, and pounds), the app gives you an instant BMI reading along with helpful context—without forcing you to create an account or stay connected to the internet.

Simple, Private & Offline-First: All BMI calculations, saved history entries, and preferences stay locally on your phone or tablet.

2. Key Features

Metric & Imperial Support

Switch easily between Metric (cm, kg) and Imperial (ft/in, lb) on the Calculator screen, or pick your default unit in Settings.

Detailed BMI Result

See your exact BMI score, category badge, visual BMI range indicator bar, healthy weight range for your height, and a weight suggestion.

Optional Age & Gender

Optionally add your age (2–120 years) and gender (Male, Female, or Unspecified) to keep clearer personal records.

Local History Log

Save results when you want, open past entries anytime to review details, delete single items, or clear all history in one tap.

Light, Dark & System Themes

Choose Light, Dark, or follow your phone's System theme with a clean Material Design 3 interface.

Adaptive Layout

Designed cleanly for both phones (bottom navigation bar) and larger screens like tablets (side navigation rail).

3. How the App Works

  • Step 1 — Enter Your Measurements: On the Calculator tab, select Metric or Imperial, enter your height and weight, and optionally add your age and gender.
  • Step 2 — Check Input Ranges: The app automatically checks your numbers to help prevent typos:
    • Metric: Height 50 – 280 cm, Weight 10 – 500 kg
    • Imperial: Total height 20 – 110 in (inches field 0 – 11.9), Weight 22 – 1100 lb
    • Age (optional): 2 – 120 years
  • Step 3 — View Your Result: Tap Calculate BMI to open the Result screen, where you can review your BMI score, category, healthy weight range, and weight suggestion.
  • Step 4 — Save to History (Optional): Tap Save Result if you want to keep a copy in your on-device History tab.

4. BMI Calculation Methodology

BMI Calculator computes your score offline on your device using standard Body Mass Index formulas:

Unit System Formula Notes
Metric BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)² Height in centimeters is converted to meters (cm ÷ 100) before squaring.
Imperial BMI = (Weight (lb) × 703) ÷ Height (in)² Total height in inches is calculated as (feet × 12) + inches.

Standard Adult BMI Categories (Ages 20+)

BMI Range Category
Below 18.5 Underweight
18.5 – 24.9 Normal
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight
30.0 – 34.9 Obesity Class 1
35.0 – 39.9 Obesity Class 2
40.0 or above Obesity Class 3

Healthy Weight Range: The healthy weight range shown for your height is calculated by solving the BMI formula for values between 18.5 and 24.9.

5. Storage, Privacy & Advertising

  • On-Device Storage: Saved BMI records are stored locally in an Android Room SQLite database (bmi_calculator_db), and your theme/unit settings are stored in Jetpack DataStore (bmi_user_preferences).
  • No Account & No Server Uploads: You do not need to sign up or log in. Your height, weight, age, gender, and BMI history are never uploaded to our servers, and Android cloud backup is disabled (android:allowBackup="false").
  • Minimal Permissions: The app only declares INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, and AD_ID for displaying Start.io banner and periodic interstitial ads when your device is online. It never accesses your camera, location, contacts, microphone, or personal files.

6. Health & Informational Disclaimer

BMI Calculator is designed for general informational and educational purposes only. BMI is a basic screening indicator and does not directly measure body fat percentage, muscle mass, fat distribution, or bone density.

Standard adult BMI categories apply to adults aged 20 and above. Athletes, muscular individuals, older adults, pregnant or nursing individuals, and children or teenagers (who require age- and sex-specific BMI-for-age percentiles) need different clinical interpretation. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
