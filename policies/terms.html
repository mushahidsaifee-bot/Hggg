2. About the App & How It Works

BMI Calculator is an offline-first Android utility app built to help you calculate your Body Mass Index (BMI), view standard adult BMI categories, check a general healthy weight range for your height, and keep a personal log of your saved calculations on your own device.

Main Features Included in the App

  • Metric & Imperial Units: Calculate BMI using centimeters and kilograms (Metric: height 50–280 cm, weight 10–500 kg) or feet, inches, and pounds (Imperial: total height 20–110 in, weight 22–1100 lb).
  • Optional Details: You can optionally enter an age (2–120 years) and pick a gender option (Male, Female, or Unspecified) for personal record-keeping.
  • BMI Formulas Used:
    • Metric: BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)²
    • Imperial: BMI = (Weight (lb) × 703) ÷ Height (in)²
  • Standard Adult BMI Categories (Ages 20+): Underweight (below 18.5), Normal (18.5 – 24.9), Overweight (25.0 – 29.9), Obesity Class 1 (30.0 – 34.9), Obesity Class 2 (35.0 – 39.9), and Obesity Class 3 (40.0 or above).
  • Healthy Weight Reference: Displays an estimated healthy weight range for your height based on BMI values between 18.5 and 24.9, along with a general weight suggestion.
  • On-Device History: You can tap Save Result to store a calculation in your local History tab, tap any saved item to review it, delete individual records, or tap Clear All to erase all saved records.
  • Appearance & Unit Preferences: Choose between System, Light, or Dark theme and set Metric or Imperial as your default measurement unit in Settings.

3. Important Health & Medical Disclaimer

Not Medical Advice: BMI Calculator is provided for general informational and educational purposes only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
  • Screening Tool Only: Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple mathematical ratio of weight to height. It does not directly measure body fat percentage, muscle mass, fat distribution, or bone density.
  • Standard Adult Categories: The BMI categories shown in the App apply to adults aged 20 and above. If you enter an age under 20, the App shows a notice reminding you of this limitation.
  • Groups Requiring Different Interpretation: Standard adult BMI ranges may not accurately reflect body composition or health risk for:
    • Children and teenagers (who require age- and sex-specific BMI-for-age percentiles).
    • Athletes and individuals with high muscle mass.
    • Older adults.
    • Pregnant or nursing individuals.
  • Consult a Professional: Always speak with a qualified doctor or healthcare professional before making any health, medical, diet, weight, or exercise decisions based on your BMI score. Never ignore professional medical advice because of something you read in this App.
